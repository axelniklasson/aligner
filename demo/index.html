Why one pixel matters

Interfaces earn trust through consistency. A button that sits a pixel lower than its neighbour, a badge that drifts two pixels right, a column header that doesn't line up with its cells — none of them register consciously, but together they make a product feel hand-assembled rather than designed.

“Alignment is the cheapest polish there is. It costs nothing but attention.”

Aligner lets you check that attention was paid, in any app, without opening an inspector.
